> [!summary] Tóm tắt
> The LinkedIn post advocates for a "shift-left" approach to data quality (DQ) management, emphasizing the critical importance of detecting and resolving data issues as early as possible within the data lifecycle. The central argument is that identifying and addressing data quality problems upstream significantly reduces the associated costs and minimizes disruptive impacts further down the data pipeline.
> 
> To achieve this, the post recommends embedding comprehensive data quality checks at every stage before data is consumed, which proactively prevents unforeseen issues. It outlines three key points for intervention:
> 1.  **Input DQ checks:** Implementing quality checks at the data ingestion phase to immediately detect errors, thereby reducing debugging efforts.
> 2.  **Mid-pipeline DQ checks:** Conducting checks during data processing to prevent quality issues from propagating and causing cascading failures throughout the pipeline.
> 3.  **Output DQ checks:** Performing final quality assessments on processed data to build confidence in data-driven decisions, ensuring the reliability and accuracy of insights.
> 
> This proactive strategy is crucial for ensuring overall data reliability and supporting robust, trustworthy decision-making processes.
